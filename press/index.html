The pitch

MiniLista does one thing beautifully: lists — shopping, packing, interview questions, monthly bills — with none of the task-manager overhead. Every action is a single tap, editing is inline and instant (like typing in Notes), and an always-visible split-view keeps every list one tap away. Built natively for iOS 26 in SwiftUI with the Liquid Glass design language, full Dynamic Type, and dark mode by default. It's free; a Premium tier adds iCloud sync, custom themes and colors, and Markdown/CSV export — with Family Sharing on both plans. Privacy-first by design: free lists never leave the device, and Premium syncs through the user's own private iCloud, which the developer can't access. No analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What makes it different

  • A point of view: pure lists — no due dates, priorities, or notifications. Restraint over feature-bloat.
  • Fast like Notes: single-tap everything; inline editing, drag to reorder.
  • Real iPad app: an always-visible split-view sidebar that scales proportionally across the whole iPad range.
  • Native iOS 26 craft: SwiftUI, Liquid Glass, full Dynamic Type, dark mode by default, zero third-party dependencies.
  • Premium: iCloud sync, custom themes & colors, Markdown/CSV export, Family Sharing.
  • Privacy-first architecture: local for free; Premium syncs via the user's private iCloud the developer can't read. No analytics, ads, or tracking.
